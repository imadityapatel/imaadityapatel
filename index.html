Aditya Patel is a researcher, academician, and technology innovator specializing in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, Software Engineering, Blockchain Technology, and Intelligent Computing Systems. With over a decade of academic and professional experience, he has consistently contributed to advancing research and innovation in emerging areas of Computer Science and Engineering.


His research focuses on developing intelligent and sustainable solutions using machine learning algorithms, predictive analytics, cloud technologies, and data-driven decision-making systems. He has published more than 25 research papers in reputed Scopus, Web of Science, UGC-indexed journals, and international conferences, covering domains such as healthcare analytics, groundwater pollution prediction, software fault prediction, blockchain security, sentiment analysis, and smart agriculture. In addition, he has authored several academic and technical books on Python Programming, Software Engineering, Blockchain, and Algorithm Design.


Aditya actively collaborates with academic institutions, industry experts, and innovation ecosystems to undertake interdisciplinary research and promote technology-driven solutions for societal challenges. He has served as a reviewer, resource person, mentor, and evaluator for various national and international conferences, faculty development programs, and innovation competitions. His current research interests include Generative AI, Explainable AI, Sustainable Computing, Intelligent IoT Systems, Cybersecurity, and Applied Machine Learning. He is committed to fostering a strong research culture, promoting innovation, and mentoring future researchers and technology leaders.
